✦ Meetings

What actually happens when we meet.

Same format every time, so newcomers always know what to expect — plus what we're reading right now.

I. How it works

The format, step by step

  1. Arrive and settle in

    Coffee, tea, and ten minutes of nothing in particular. No one's tracking who's "caught up."

  2. Read a passage together

    We read a key section out loud, round-robin and always optional, so the book is fresh for everyone in the room.

  3. Open discussion

    Guided by a few loose questions — never a quiz, never a test of comprehension.

  4. Pick up the next chunk

    We agree on a short, doable amount for next time and hand out any copies that are needed.
